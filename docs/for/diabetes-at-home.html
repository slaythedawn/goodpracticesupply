Small decisions that add up

01
A new needle every time
Reusing a pen needle bends the tip into a hook, and a hooked tip tears rather than pierces. It is the single biggest cause of lumpy injection sites over years.
02
Rotate, and keep rotating
Same spot every day builds lipohypertrophy, and insulin absorbs unpredictably from scarred tissue. A rotation you actually follow beats a perfect one you do not.
03
Buy the bigger box
A box of 100 is not much more than a box of 30 and it removes the Sunday night problem entirely. Always Stocked will ship it before you run low if you would rather not think about it.
04
Check the NDSS first
If you are registered, some of this is subsidised and you should use that. We are for what falls outside it, or for when you need something today.
