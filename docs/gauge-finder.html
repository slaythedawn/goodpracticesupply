Gauge, explained

Two numbers, doing two different jobs.

Gauge is how thick the needle is, and the scale runs backwards. Length is how deep it goes. Get both right and you will barely feel it.

Thickness
Higher number, thinner needle
31G is finer than 29G. Thinner is more comfortable but slower to fill. Thicker draws faster, which matters for a large or viscous dose.
29G, fills fastest
30G, the middle
31G, least sensation
Length
Shorter is safer under the skin
Australian diabetes guidance favours the shortest length available, because a short needle rarely reaches muscle whatever your size. 6mm is the shortest on the national schedule.
